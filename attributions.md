# Generative AI Use
 
- Tool: Claude (Claude Sonnet 5.5), made by Anthropic, used through claude.ai
- Date: October 3, 2026
- What it was used for: Generating the contents of `items.json`, a 16-item array of music albums and EPs following the assignment's provided JSON schema (`id`, `name`, `image`, `alt`, `category`, `description`).
- Prompt: I used the data-generation prompt template provided for the assignment, with the topic changed to my own favorite albums and EPs.
- What I supplied: The list of 16 albums/EPs (title and artist for each).
- What the AI generated: The `category` groupings, the one-sentence `description` for each item and the `alt` text. I also used ChatGPT's GPT-6, developed by OpenAI, to generate sample comments for all 16 albums and EPs in comments.json, replacing the original comments with ones more relevant to my music website.
